# - Dictionary terms are marked [[Term]]. In [[Term|spoken]], `spoken` is only what text-to-speech reads so that it
#   says the term the way a person would; the reference text and the recording prompt show `Term`. A term is spoken as
#   the Whisper probe spoke it: `spoken` in evals/terms.toml, or nothing.
# - {pause 2.5} is a pause of 2.5 seconds (long entries only).
# - A line starting with whitespace continues the entry above it.
# - Ids: en-NNN are ordinary sentences, en-long-NN (35–90 s with pauses) and en-short-NN are must-pass cases.
#   Odd numbers go to the tune split and even numbers to holdout. Ids are stable: add new entries at the end. The
#   entries before en-073, en-long-06 and en-short-07 named real products and were retired; their ids aren't reused.
# - No numbers: the reference has to match what Whisper writes after normalisation, and it writes digits.
# - Every term invented for the eval set (evals/terms.toml), and every word outside the marks either common or listed
#   in words.txt. `scripts/synth-evals.sh --check` enforces these rules, the splits and the coverage of each term
#   class; see check_rules in scripts/lib/synth_evals.py.
